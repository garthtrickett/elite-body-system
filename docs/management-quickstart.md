# EBS management — team quick start

Open **EBS Master** at `/master/sign-in`. The isolated editing preview uses
`harrybaldwin`. Production uses the same username with a secure link to the owner's
configured email. Staff access is granted explicitly; client accounts cannot open
management pages.

## Elite Marketing System

1. Choose **Start an article**.
2. Select a YouTube source and explain its relevance to the EBS client.
3. Answer the six interview questions. Type, record or upload a voice note.
4. Give the real personal/client result or story supporting the point.
5. Choose **Build blog and script**. Review the language and check the facts.
6. Add pictures, save, then download for Notion or as a branded HTML document.

Voice failures can be retried from the same browser. Closing the page removes an
untranscribed recording. Save written drafts before leaving or switching articles.
Nothing publishes automatically.

## Elite Sales System

- **Pipeline:** search for a lead, read the application and journey, update the
  stage and save a clear next action in Private notes.
- **Funnel pages:** edit Free Value, the supplied application website, booking
  introduction/FAQs and client stories. Save changes and refresh the public page.
- **Call availability:** add the times you can actually take calls. The app
  reserves one slot per confirmed booking and provides a calendar download.
- **Call script:** open it from a lead to bring their application into the CLOSER
  conversation. Explain the agreed support options during the call.
- Mark **Client** only after enrolment is confirmed. This does not collect payment
  or create their member account automatically.

Public journey: `/free-value` → `/apply` → `/book-call` → `/client-stories`.
Signup opens the editable 30-day web guide. Automatic insight emails are off.
Only stories marked Publish with confirmed permission appear in the story feed.
Add the actual introduction, FAQ and case-study video URLs when available.

## Elite Delivery

- **EBS Mentor Portal:** search for a client, then open Overview, Messages,
  Progress or Programming. Expand the Start Point Form to read the full answers.
- **Add a confirmed member:** enter the member's email and agreed coaching option.
  Existing members keep their current membership. This does not send an invitation
  or process payment.
- **Group & community** enables the shared community. **Individual access** also
  enables private coaching messages.
- **Programming:** choose the session and effective date, adjust its prescription
  and write clear instructions. New sessions use those changes; logged history
  keeps its original prescription.
- **Check-ins:** read the submission, request the private AI summary if useful,
  and mark reviewed. The item leaves the queue and stays in the member's history.
- **EBS Member Portal:** opens your signed-in account's client experience. This
  does not impersonate another member.

After an editing session, batch verification and CI before merging and deploying.
During local preview editing, refresh the page to see updates.
